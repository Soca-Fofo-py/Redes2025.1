For 2 digit numbers: The tens place is filled in 9 ways, the units place is the same as the tens - hence 9 numbers

For 3 digit numbers: the first and last digit can be filled in 9 ways and the middle name in 10 ways, hence 90

For 4 digit numbers: the first and last digit can be filled in 9 ways. The second place in 10 ways and the third place is the same as the second, hence total = 9 x 10 = 90 ways.

For 5 digit numbers: the first and last digit in 9 ways; the second and fourth place in 10 ways; the middle number in 10 ways, hence 9x10x10 = 900 ways

So 9+90+90+900 = 1089 ways
